The Knight Bride

The Knight Bride is a standalone romantasy book set in a pseudo-Renaissance fantasy kingdom of Remaly.

Crown Prince Leonard has a problem: despite his father having been dead for over two years, he still has not been crowned. The law is clear—he must be married to a woman before he can claim the throne. Unfortunately, his fiancées have a troubling habit of dying before the wedding.

When he meets the young knight Sir Gard, Leonard devises a desperate solution. Gard can pose as his bride—and, as a trained knight, stand a far better chance of surviving the attempts on his life. It seems like the perfect plan… until it becomes clear that Sir Gard is not what he appears to be.

His name is not Lysander, but Lysandra. And both of them already have complicated attachments: Leonard to his lover Adrien, and Lysandra to her long-standing affection for her best friend, Sir Nicholas Greyville.

The Knight Bride is a humorous, queer and polyamorous romantasy story about secret identities, tangled loyalties, and a love story that refuses to follow the expected path.
